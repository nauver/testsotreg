The local pulse of Europe

What regions and cities tell us

Europe’s strength is built in its cities, towns and regions, where shared challenges become practical solutions.

This page transforms key messages and local examples from the report into a readable testimonial format. Quotations are editorial adaptations for the infographic, not verbatim statements by named individuals.

Three lenses

Cohesion. Resilience. Proximity.

  • 3Proximity

Six places. Six transformations.

Industrial change, affordable housing, biodiversity, clean energy and public services look different in every territory. These examples show how European ambitions become local action.

Competitiveness

Limerick, Ireland

“Investment in semiconductor research and manufacturing can create skilled jobs and anchor Europe’s digital transformation in its regions.”

The report highlights a EUR 630 million investment expected to create 600 jobs.

◆

Local meaning

Just transition

Silesia, Poland

“A fair transition means giving workers, businesses and communities a credible path beyond coal.”

The local strategy combines reskilling, economic diversification, research and innovation.

↗

Affordable housing

Prague, Czechia

“Affordable, energy-efficient homes help essential workers remain part of the city they serve.”

More than 700 affordable rental apartments are planned for public-sector workers.

⌂

Local meaning

Biodiversity

From evidence to action

Three principles for place-based policy

01

Start with place

Recognise different starting points, assets and pressures instead of applying one uniform response.

02

Connect investment

Align skills, services, infrastructure and innovation so that projects generate lasting local value.

03

Work with communities

Build policies with the public authorities, organisations and residents responsible for delivery.

A shared path

How local action becomes European progress

Listen

Identify territorial needs through local evidence and direct participation.

Design

Combine European ambition with realistic local delivery conditions.

Invest

Connect finance with capacity, skills, infrastructure and public services.

Learn

Share results between territories and adapt approaches to new contexts.

A shared direction

Listen locally. Act together.

Place-based policy begins by recognising that every territory has different strengths, pressures and starting points. Europe’s common ambitions become credible when regions and cities help shape and deliver them.
